# Personal learning reflection - prompts

Write these yourself, in your own words, from your own experience. The questions are the
learning-diary questions from the course overview (MM-AI-01, p. 21), plus a few project-specific ones.
Honest notes about what did not work are as valuable as successes.

## Course questions (roughly weekly)
1. What have I learned in the last weeks?
2. How did the lecture (slides) and practical training help with learning?
3. How did I find other information sources, and how long did it take to find them?
4. How was my communication with team members and the lecturer?
5. How far did I get with my project tasks? Am I still on track?
6. How effective was my work / learning last week? What can I improve?
7. What are my next tasks and knowledge gaps to tackle? Do I have uncertainties?
8. How did lecture, practical training and project work play together?

## Prompts specific to Steps 1-2
* What did I expect each dataset to be like, and what surprised me once I looked at real samples?
* Which part of the dataset access (licences, download sizes, formats) was harder than expected?
* What did I learn about WER vs CER, and corpus-level vs mean per-recording scores?
* Which normalisation choice changed the numbers most, and do I think it was the right choice?
* What practical problems did I hit with the environment (hardware, libraries, security settings), and what did I learn from solving them?
* Which errors of the model did I find most interesting, and what do I want to test in Step 3?
* What would I do differently if I started Step 2 again?
